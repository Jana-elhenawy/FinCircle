WHAT CAN YOU INVEST IN? — LEARN THE BASICS
🥇
Gold Fund
Low Risk
A pool of money invested in gold. Protects against inflation — when the Egyptian pound weakens, gold value typically rises. Good for beginners.
Historical return+11.2% YTD
Min. to start1 EGP
⚠️ Risk: Gold prices fluctuate daily. Not guaranteed returns.
🏛️
Treasury Bills (T-Bills)
Very Low Risk
You lend money to the Egyptian government for 91–364 days. The government pays you back with interest. Almost zero default risk — backed by the state.
Current rate27.5% p.a.
Min. to start50 EGP
💡 Best for: Short-term savings you don't need for 3–6 months.
📊
EGX30 Index Fund
Medium Risk
Instead of picking one company, you invest in Egypt's top 30 companies at once. If the Egyptian economy grows, your investment grows too. Diversified = safer than single stocks.
5-yr avg return+8.7% / yr
Min. to start10 EGP
⚠️ Risk: Can lose value in market downturns. Invest only what you can keep for 1+ year.
🏠
Real Estate Fund
Medium-High Risk
Pool money with others to invest in Egyptian property developments. You earn from rental income and property appreciation without buying a full apartment.
Historical return+15.3% YTD
Min. to start100 EGP
💡 Liquidity: Harder to cash out quickly. Think 2–5 year horizon.
